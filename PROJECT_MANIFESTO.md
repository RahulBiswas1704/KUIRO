### 1. Business Knowledge (The Brand Brain)
* **Beyond Prompts**: Businesses do not have time to engineer prompts. KUIRO crawls the client's website, Google Business Profile, and collateral once, extracting core services, customer personas, visual hex codes, logos, and negative guardrails.
* **Persistent Memory**: Every piece of content is anchored in the client's genuine commercial context.

### 2. Regional & Cultural Context (The 365-Day Engine)
* **Hyper-Local Relevance**: India and global emerging markets celebrate dozens of regional festivals (Durga Puja in Bengal, Pongal in Tamil Nadu, Ganesh Chaturthi in Maharashtra, Bihu in Assam, Chhath Puja in Bihar).
* **Zero Cookie-Cutter Templates**: Rather than slapping a logo onto a generic stock image used by 10,000 other businesses, KUIRO synthesizes the festival's spiritual and cultural essence directly with the brand's unique offering.

### 3. Branded Content (Programmatic Compositor)
* **Studio-Grade Aesthetics**: Generates raw, high-resolution AI visual canvases (Flux.1 / Imagen 3) without warped text, then overlays high-DPI typography, badges, contact numbers, and verified SVG logos using `@vercel/og` (Satori) and Sharp.

### 4. Frictionless Approvals (48-Hour Auto-Commit via WhatsApp)
* **The Passive Protocol**: Business owners never have to log into a web dashboard to approve posts. 
* **T-48 Hours**: The admin receives an interactive WhatsApp preview card.
* **Silence = Consent**: If no action is taken, KUIRO auto-commits and publishes at the algorithmically optimal hour.
* **Voice-First Revisions**: The admin can send a quick WhatsApp voice note in their native tongue: *"Make the headline highlight our 20% discount on health checkups."* KUIRO transcribes the audio and re-delivers the asset in 15 seconds.

### 5. Multi-Platform Publishing & Inbound RAG
* **Official OAuth 2.0 Compliance**: Zero headless browser scraping; resilient retry queues; automated token refreshes.
* **Inbound Comment-to-DM**: Converts post engagement into WhatsApp inquiries and handles questions automatically using a vector knowledge base.
